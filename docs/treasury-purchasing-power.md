# Treasury Purchasing Power Engine

How Sozu measures, projects, and displays the full economic impact of holding USDC for LatAm SMBs — beyond APY alone.

---

## Executive summary

For a merchant in Chile, Argentina, or Brazil, the most damaging financial loss is usually not *missing DeFi yield* — it is **currency debasement**. A business account sitting in CLP, ARS, or BRL silently loses purchasing power every day due to inflation and local currency depreciation against the dollar.

Sozu's wallet is designed as:

> An operating account that **protects and grows** your business money.

To make that promise tangible, Sozu shows not just "APY" but a **Treasury Performance** breakdown — the complete picture of what holding USDC in a yield-bearing strategy actually did for your purchasing power, measured in the currency you spend in.

---

## Canonical vs reference currency

**USDC is the canonical state of every Sozu wallet.** It is the on-chain source of truth. Every on-chain operation, balance record, and yield calculation is denominated in USDC.

CLP, ARS, BRL, COP, and other fiat currencies are **reference layers** — visual translations that help users understand their purchasing power in familiar terms. They are never the settlement or accounting unit.

This distinction matters both philosophically and technically:

| Property | USDC (canonical) | Local fiat (reference) |
|----------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Source of truth | Yes | No |
| On-chain balance | Yes | No |
| Yield calculation | In USDC | Converted from USDC |
| Inflation metric | N/A — dollar-backed | Applied to show purchasing power impact |
| FX metric | N/A | USDXXX rate movement |

When users see "CLP" on the balance card, the subtitle makes this explicit: *"Referencia · no es saldo en CLP"*.

## APY vs projected earnings

**APY (Annual Percentage Yield)** is a standardized annualized rate that assumes:
- the position stays open for a full year
- compounding occurs at the stated frequency
- the APY rate remains constant throughout

Users do not think in APY. Users think: *"How much did I actually earn?"*

Sozu shows both:

| Display | What it means |
|---------|--------------|
| Protocol APY (e.g. `8.19% APY`) | Blend strategy annualized yield — purely the DeFi component |
| Projected period earnings (`+$18.20`) | Dollar/local-currency amount earned in a specific holding window |
| Purchasing power impact (`+14.99% est.`) | Combined yield + inflation + FX benefit, annualized — the real economic impact |

The combined metric is labeled *"Estimado"* or *"Proyección"* throughout the UI. It is never described as a guaranteed return.

## Three value layers

### 1. DeFi Yield

Productive capital growth generated by the Blend/DeFindex strategy.

- Source: `lib/defindex/apy-calculator.ts` → `getRealTimeAPY()` → Blend API → fallback 15.5%
- Compounding frequency: daily (continuous approximation)
- This is real, earned yield. It is the most conservative and reliable number.

**Formula:**

```
yieldUsd = balanceUsdc * (effectiveApy / 100) * (days / 365)
           [for simple projection; daily compound = balanceUsdc * ((1 + apy/100/365)^days - 1)]
```

### 2. Inflation avoidance

The purchasing power that would have been lost if the merchant had held local fiat instead of USDC.

This is *not* yield. It is an estimated avoided loss — the counterfactual cost of holding CLP/ARS/etc.

- Source: Phase 1 — static CPI table per country; Phase 2 — live CPI API or curated monthly feed
- Expressed as local-currency equivalent of avoided loss

**Formula:**

```
inflationAvoidedLocal = balanceLocal * (annualInflationRate / 100) * (days / 365)
```

where `balanceLocal = balanceUsdc * spotFxRate`.

### 3. FX protection

The purchasing power gained when the local currency weakens against the dollar over the holding period.

- Source: Phase 1 — mock period change table; Phase 2 — historical FX snapshots from `/api/fx` or Frankfurter
- Also *not* yield: it is a currency appreciation effect, and it can be negative if USD weakens.

**Formula:**

```
fxChangePercent = (fxRateNow / fxRateAtStart) - 1
fxProtectionLocal = balanceLocal * fxChangePercent
```

### Total impact

```
totalAmountLocal = (yieldUsd * fxRateNow) + inflationAvoidedLocal + fxProtectionLocal
totalPercentPeriod = totalAmountLocal / balanceLocal
totalPercentAnnualized = totalPercentPeriod * (365 / days)
```

**Compliance note:** The total is an *estimated purchasing power impact*, not a return on investment. The UI always includes a disclaimer; inflation and FX components are probabilistic, not contractual.

## Projection engine inputs and outputs

### Inputs

| Field | Type | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| `balanceUsdc` | `number` | Current total USDC balance (wallet + strategy) |
| `referenceFiat` | `"CLP" \| "ARS" \| "BRL" \| "COP"` | User's home currency (from preferences) |
| `mode` | `"efficient" \| "balanced" \| "fast"` | Treasury mode (affects effective APY and cashout cadence) |
| `holdingDays` | `number` | Projection window in days (7, 14, 30, 90) |
| `protocolApy` | `number` | Live or fallback APY from Blend (percent) |
| `spotFxRate` | `number` | Current USDXXX rate |
| `fxChangePct` | `number` | Period change in USDXXX (e.g. +2.3% means local currency weakened) |
| `annualInflation` | `number` | Annual CPI for reference fiat (percent) |

### Treasury modes

| Mode | Description | Effective APY haircut | Suggested cashout |
|------|-------------|----------------------|-------------------|
| Efficient | Maximize purchasing power; maximize DeFi allocation | 0% (full APY) | 90+ days |
| Balanced | Predictable weekly liquidity | −10% relative | 30 days |
| Fast | Daily operational cashouts; low DeFi allocation | −30% relative | 7 days |

The haircut models reduced time-in-strategy when the user cashes out frequently. Phase 2 can calibrate this against real deposit/withdrawal data.

### Output: `TreasuryProjection`

```typescript
type TreasuryProjection = {
  periodDays: number
  referenceFiat: "CLP" | "ARS" | "BRL" | "COP"
  protocolApy: number       // e.g. 8.19 — raw Blend APY
  merchantApy: number       // after mode haircut
  layers: {
    yield: { percent: number; amountUsd: number; amountLocal: number }
    inflationAvoided: { percent: number; amountLocal: number }
    fxProtection: { percent: number; amountLocal: number }
  }
  total: {
    percentAnnualized: number  // e.g. 15.6
    percentPeriod: number      // e.g. 1.21
    amountLocal: number        // e.g. 36300 CLP ≈ +$36.30 eq.
  }
  comparison: {
    localFiatLossPercent: number  // inflation + fx cost of holding local fiat
  }
  disclaimer: string
  dataSource: "mock" | "live"
}
```

## Data sources roadmap

| Input | Phase 1 (mock) | Phase 2 (live) |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| Blend APY | Existing `getRealTimeAPY()` + 15.5% fallback | Same (Blend API already partially wired) |

| FX period change | Static mock % per fiat per period | Store FX snapshot at first load; compute delta vs current; or use Frankfurter historical endpoint |
| Inflation (CPI) | Static annual rate table in `lib/treasury/mock-inflation.ts` | IMF WEO, national statistics (BCCh for CLP, INDEC for ARS, IBGE for BRL, DANE for COP), or curated monthly CPI feed |
| Holding period | User preference in `localStorage` (key `sozu_treasury_prefs:v1`) | Persist to Supabase `wallet_preferences` table (Phase 2 migration) |
| Treasury mode | User preference in `localStorage` | Same |

## API contract

### `GET /api/wallet/treasury/projection`

**Auth:** Requires valid Supabase session (same pattern as `/api/wallet/defindex/apy`).

**Query parameters:**

| Param | Type | Default | Description |
|-------|------|---------|-------------|
| `balance` | `number` | required | USDC balance to project against |
| `referenceFiat` | `string` | `CLP` | Reference fiat currency |
| `mode` | `string` | `balanced` | Treasury mode: `efficient`, `balanced`, `fast` |
| `holdingDays` | `number` | `30` | Projection window in days |

**Response (200):**

```jsonc
{
  "success": true,
  "projection": {
    "periodDays": 30,
    "referenceFiat": "CLP",
    "protocolApy": 8.19,
    "merchantApy": 7.37,
    "layers": {
      "yield":            { "percent": 0.60, "amountUsd": 18.20, "amountLocal": 17290 },
      "inflationAvoided": { "percent": 0.37, "amountLocal": 11400 },
      "fxProtection":     { "percent": 0.19, "amountLocal": 6700  }
    },
    "total": {
      "percentAnnualized": 15.6,
      "percentPeriod": 1.21,
      "amountLocal": 35390
    },
    "comparison": {
      "localFiatLossPercent": -0.80
    },
    "disclaimer": "Estimaciones basadas en tasas históricas y datos mock. No garantizado.",

  }

```

## UI copy guidelines

### Safe wording

| Context | Use | Avoid |
|---------|-----|-------|
| Yield layer | "Rendimiento Blend", "Yield generado" | "Ganancia garantizada", "Retorno asegurado" |
| Inflation layer | "Inflación estimada evitada", "Poder adquisitivo preservado (est.)" | "Protección garantizada contra inflación" |
| FX layer | "Protección cambiaria estimada" | "Ganancia por tipo de cambio" |
| Total | "Impacto estimado en poder adquisitivo", "Proyección de tesorería" | "Rendimiento total", "APR total" |
| Disclaimer | "Estimado · no garantizado · basado en tasas históricas" | Omitting disclaimer from any screen showing the total |

### Comparison copy

When showing the "vs holding CLP" comparison:

> "Mantener este saldo en CLP probablemente habría reducido tu poder adquisitivo ~0.8% este mes (estimado, basado en inflación e IPC histórico)."

Always mark as estimated. Do not say "would have lost" — say "probablemente habría reducido".

### Balance card reference fiat note

> "Referencia · no es saldo en CLP"

This note must appear whenever the balance is displayed in a reference fiat to prevent users from treating the converted amount as their actual balance.

## Treasury modes — future behavior

| Mode | Who it's for | DeFi allocation | Cashout cadence | Trade-off |
|------|-------------|-----------------|-----------------|-----------|
| Efficient | Businesses with predictable, infrequent cash needs | 100% in strategy | Every 90+ days | Maximum yield; less liquidity |
| Balanced | Most SMBs; predictable monthly payroll/supplier cycles | ~80% in strategy | Every 30 days | Good balance of yield + access |
| Fast | High-frequency merchants; daily POS reconciliation | ~50–60% in strategy | Every 7 days | Immediate access; lower yield |

In Phase 2, modes can auto-suggest based on transaction history cadence from the email ledger.
